# Electrician Website Template

A reusable, production-ready static website for electricians and electrical contractors, built to the Rank Boss master website specification (technical SEO, local SEO, schema, accessibility, legal drafts).

The home page opens on a **house that gets wired as you scroll**: power comes in from the pole, the meter starts spinning, the panel energizes and its breakers flip on, then circuit by circuit a live wire traces from the panel through the joists to each room and the room lights up: kitchen, living room, bedrooms, basement (with the sump pump), the garage (where the EV starts charging) and outside (soffits and the hot tub). The camera glides from room to room, a HUD counts circuits and amps, big messages play along the way ("Safe for your family.", "Ready for your EV.") and the instant estimate card arrives at the end. It's inline SVG driven by a small script, with no libraries and no outside requests. With reduced motion or no JavaScript, the hero is a normal hero with the house drawn fully powered.

It also includes an **instant electrical estimate** for six jobs (EV charger, panel upgrade, pot lights, generator hookup, hot tub wiring, outlets and circuits), each with its own questions, priced from the client's own numbers.

Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No frameworks, no tracking and no third-party requests: fonts are self-hosted and every drawing is inline SVG generated by the build.

Demo: `rankboss-electrician.pages.dev` (noindexed). Same engine as the fence, HVAC and roofing templates, so tweaks carry over.

```

/assets/css/main.css      all styles (design tokens at the top; OPENING ANIMATION and HOUSE SCROLL SCENE sections)
/assets/js/main.js        site interactions, intro, menus, forms (no dependencies)
/assets/js/house.js       the house wired as you scroll (home page only)
/assets/js/estimate.js    the instant electrical estimate
/assets/fonts/            Anton, Barlow, Barlow Condensed, JetBrains Mono (SIL Open Font License)
/assets/img/              logo, favicons, social image (generated by make_images.py)
/_build/                  generator, content and QA — NOT published
  site.config.json        ← client details, colours, services, licence number, intro, house scene and ESTIMATE PRICING
  content_services.py     ← service page copy + per-city paragraphs
  content_costs.py        ← "what affects the cost" and "questions to ask" per service
  content_cities.py       ← service-area cities: local housing, wiring and permits
  pages.py                ← home, about, FAQ and legal copy (incl. the house scene's circuits and messages)
  electric_art.py         ← every drawing: the house, the panel schedule, estimate previews, hero lines
  build.py                ← generator (header, footer, nav, schema, sitemap, estimate page)
  qa.py                   ← static checks (links, SEO, schema, word counts, contrast, estimate wiring…)
  qa_browser.py           ← browser checks (intro, house scene, every estimate job's maths, forms, phones, axe)
```

**Never hand-edit the generated HTML.** Change `_build/`, then rebuild.

## Setting up a new client

1. Edit `_build/site.config.json`: business name, legal name, domain, phone, email, hours, address (or `null` for a service-area business), Google Business Profile, social profiles.
2. Brand and offer:
   - `services`: delete any slug the client doesn't offer. Its pages, menu and footer links, sitemap entries, form options and **its estimate job** disappear. Fix any sentences listed in `_build/removed-service-mentions.txt` (QA fails while it exists).
   - `accent` (main brand colour) and `accent_alt` (the "live circuit" second colour), both `#rrggbb`. The build stops if either is too dark to read.
   - `logo`: client logo in `assets/img/client/` (transparent PNG best).
   - `licence_number`: the client's ESA Licensed Electrical Contractor number. It then shows in the footer and the about page. Leave it empty until confirmed; nothing is shown and nothing is claimed.
   - `emergency` / `emergency_note`: set `emergency` to `false` if the client doesn't take after-hours calls (removes the urgent call lines).
   - `photos`: the client's own job photos only (see `_build/photos.py`).
   - `house_scene`: `false` turns off the scroll scene (the home page then opens on a normal hero with circuit lines). Its words (circuits, messages) are in `pages.py` → `house`.
3. **Estimate pricing** (`estimator` block), the client's real numbers. Each job has a `base` price and `inputs`:
   - `range`: a slider, priced `per_unit` above `free_units` (e.g. EV charger: $14/ft beyond the first 15 ft).
   - `count`: a stepper, priced `per_unit` each (e.g. dimmers, new outlets).
   - `choice`: radio options, each with `add` (dollars added) and/or `mult` (multiplies the job's range and count work), and an optional `note` shown in the result.
   - `check`: a checkbox with `add`; `service` hides it when the client doesn't offer that service.
   - `permit_included`, `range_pct`, `minimum_job`, `round_to`; `enabled: false` removes the tool, its nav links, hero picker and legal paragraphs.
4. `form_endpoint`: FormSubmit (`https://formsubmit.co/CLIENT-EMAIL`). The first submission sends an activation email that must be confirmed.
5. Cities changed? Rewrite `content_cities.py` and each service's `"cities"` text. Keep it genuinely local.
6. Review everything marked `CLIENT REVIEW` (HTML comments and config notes): hero badges, working standards, about page, emergency and commercial claims, legal drafts.
7. Build and test:
   ```bash
   pip install pillow fonttools brotli playwright
   python3 _build/build.py
   python3 _build/qa.py
   python3 _build/qa_browser.py --axe path/to/axe.min.js   # axe optional: npm pack axe-core
   ```
8. At launch: set `"domain"` to the client's domain and `"demo_mode": false` (removes noindex and the yellow demo banner).

## The house scroll scene: how it works

The home hero is pinned for about six screens. `house.js` turns scroll position into progress `p` (0–1): 0–0.07 hero copy over the dark house; 0.07–0.15 the service drop, meter and feed light up; 0.15–0.22 the panel energizes; 0.22–0.88 six circuits in turn, each wire tracing from the panel (`--w` 0→1) and then its room lighting up with a flicker (`.is-lit`); after 0.9 everything is on, the final message shows and the estimate card appears. The camera is the SVG's `viewBox`, gliding between a focus point per circuit (on phones it zooms in closer and keeps the room below the messages). The HUD shows the circuit, a running amp count, progress and a strip of breakers. The drawing is in `electric_art.house_svg()`; circuit ids there must match `pages.py` → `house.circuits`.

## Opening animation: "power up"

About 3 seconds: a breaker panel fades in out of the dark, twelve breakers flip on one by one with their status lights coming on, the main breaker throws with a flash, sparks and a jolt, the business name flickers on like a tube light, then light floods the screen and the page appears. Pure CSS (timings in `main.css`, OPENING ANIMATION).

It plays on **whichever page a visitor opens first** in a browser session, then never replays that visit. It's skipped for a `#hash`, for reduced-motion users and on the 404 and thank-you pages, and a tap or key press skips it. `"intro_scope": "home"` limits it to the home page; `"intro": false` turns it off.

## Design

Blue-black night with a safety-yellow accent and an arc-blue "live" colour (both from config). Anton headlines, Barlow body, JetBrains Mono for readouts. Blueprint-line drawings: the cut-away house, a panel schedule, and live previews in the estimate (pot lights that light up as you add them, an EV charging, breakers filling a panel). Everything respects `prefers-reduced-motion`. No giant business name in the footer.

## Deploying

Cloudflare Pages (like the other Rank Boss demos): connect the repo, no build command, output directory `/`. `_headers` noindexes the `*.pages.dev` hostname only.

## Adding a page later

- New service: add an entry to `content_services.py` (with its three `cities` paragraphs), a `COSTS` and `ASK` entry in `content_costs.py`, add the slug to `services` in the config, rebuild, run QA.
- New city: add an entry to `content_cities.py` and a paragraph for it in every service's `cities`, rebuild, run QA.

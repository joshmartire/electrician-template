Knob & Tube Replacement for LaSalle homes

LaSalle grew quickly from the 1990s on, so most calls here are about adding capacity: EV chargers, hot tubs, pools and finished basements. Knob and tube is rare in LaSalle's newer subdivisions, but older homes along the river and in the town's original core can still have it. Buyers moving into those homes often call us after an insurer asks the question, and the paperwork from the replacement answers it.

What's included

Removal and replacement of knob and tube wiring in older homes, with documentation for insurance and home sales. Depending on your property, the work can include:

  • Tracing every circuit
  • New grounded wiring
  • Disconnecting the old system
  • Careful access
  • Paperwork
  • Assessment.
  • Written quote.
  • Replacement.
  • Inspection and paperwork.

Full details, how the work runs and common questions are on our main knob & tube replacement page.

Local conditions in LaSalle

LaSalle's newer drywall construction makes adding pot lights, outlets and basement circuits straightforward. Larger homes mean longer runs to backyards, decks and garages, and plenty of basements to finish with bars, gyms, theatres and guest suites.

Questions to ask before you hire

Whoever you hire for knob & tube replacement in LaSalle, get clear answers to these before signing:

  • How much knob and tube is still live?
  • Will the old system be disconnected completely?
  • Is patching included?
  • What's your ESA licence number, and will you file the permit?
  • Will you provide paperwork for my insurer?

Permits and inspections

Electrical work in LaSalle needs a permit (a notification) filed with the Electrical Safety Authority, and paid work must be done by an ESA Licensed Electrical Contractor. ESA inspects the work and issues a Certificate of Acceptance. Hot tubs, pools and EV chargers are all permitted work. Service upgrades also need the local utility for the disconnect and reconnect, and buried runs need utility locates before digging.

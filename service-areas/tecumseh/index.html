Permits and inspections in Tecumseh

Electrical work in Tecumseh needs a permit (a notification) filed with the Electrical Safety Authority, and paid work must be done by an ESA Licensed Electrical Contractor. ESA inspects the work and issues a Certificate of Acceptance. Feeds to shops and outbuildings are permitted work too. Service upgrades also need the local utility for the disconnect and reconnect, and buried runs need utility locates before digging.

How the instant estimate works

Each job starts from Voltline Electric Co.'s typical price for that work. The tool adds the options you choose, such as the distance from your panel, the number of fixtures, your panel size and extras, then shows the result as a range. The ESA permit and inspection are included in the price.

Measure distances along the route a wire would actually take, along walls and through the basement, not in a straight line. If you're not sure of your panel size, look at the number on the main breaker at the top of the panel, or choose "Not sure".

What each job includes

  • EV charger — includes a dedicated 240 V circuit and breaker, wire run, receptacle or hard-wired connection, ESA permit and inspection.
  • Panel upgrade — includes the new panel and breakers, labelling, grounding and bonding, coordination with the utility, ESA permit and inspection.
  • Pot lights — includes slim LED pot lights, wiring and switching, patching of the access holes we make, ESA permit and inspection.
  • Generator hookup — includes the transfer equipment, inlet or connection, wire run, ESA permit and inspection.
  • Hot tub wiring — includes the dedicated circuit and breaker, a GFCI-protected disconnect at the tub, bonding, ESA permit and inspection.
  • Outlets & circuits — includes devices, boxes and cover plates, wiring from the nearest suitable circuit or the panel, ESA permit and inspection.

Why the final price can differ

Some costs can't be known without seeing the house: the condition and capacity of the panel, how the wire can be routed, plaster or finished ceilings, what's found inside the walls, code updates that become required when work is done, and any utility charges. That's why the estimate is a range and your actual price is confirmed in a written quote after a site visit. For rewiring, renovations and commercial work, contact us for a quote.

Instant estimate questions

Is the instant estimate a quote?

No. It's a ballpark based on the options you choose. Your actual price comes in a written quote after a site visit.

Do you see my choices when I use the tool?

No. The estimate is calculated in your browser, and nothing is sent to us unless you submit the form.

Does the estimate include utility charges?

No. Where a job needs the utility, such as a service upgrade, any utility charges are confirmed separately.
